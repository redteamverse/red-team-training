Training Categories

Red Team Training Across Enterprise Environments

01 Active Directory

Active Directory Training for Red & Blue Teams

Practice attacking and defending Active Directory in hands-on labs. Work through domain enumeration, privilege escalation, Kerberos attacks and trust abuse, and see how Active Directory activity is monitored and attacks are detected.

02 Azure Cloud

Azure Cloud Training for Red & Blue Teams

Practice Azure Red Teaming and defense in live Azure tenants and hybrid infrastructure. Work through reconnaissance, initial access, enumeration, privilege escalation, lateral movement and persistence.

03 Enterprise AI

Enterprise AI Red Teaming

Practice attacking and defending Copilot, Copilot Studio, Microsoft Foundry and AI agents across Microsoft 365 and Azure in live labs. Work through agent identity abuse, guardrail evasion, privilege escalation and lateral movement.
