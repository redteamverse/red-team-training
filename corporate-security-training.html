Corporate Training FAQ

Questions About Training Your Security Team

Find answers about training topics, delivery options, team access, labs, and certifications.

Have another question? Share it with our team through the corporate training form.

What topics does the corporate training cover?

Training covers Active Directory, Windows, Azure, and enterprise AI security. The topics included will depend on the course or training program you choose.

Can the training be adapted for our team?

Yes. The program can be planned around your team’s roles, skill levels, security priorities, schedule, and preferred training format.

What training formats are available?

Options may include self-paced training, instructor-led sessions, private team cohorts, onsite training, and virtual training. Available formats vary by program.

Is the training suitable for different skill levels?

Yes. Training is available for foundational and advanced skill levels. We can help you select a suitable path based on your team’s current experience.

Does the training include hands-on labs?

Yes. Team members practice techniques in enterprise lab environments. Lab access and infrastructure depend on the selected course.

Can we monitor our team’s progress?

Yes. Security leaders can manage learner access, monitor progress, review exam activity, and transfer seat through the team platform.

Are certification exams included?

Selected courses include a practical certification exam attempt. Certification options depend on the training program you choose.

How do we choose the right program?

Tell us about your team size, experience, security priorities, and preferred format. We will recommend an existing training path or discuss a tailored program.
